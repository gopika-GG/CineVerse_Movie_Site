PopcornBits Says

By creating a PopcornBits account, you agree to these key conditions:

  • You must be at least 18 years old and provide accurate signup information.
  • Keep your account credentials secure and be responsible for activity on your account.
  • Use PopcornBits lawfully and respectfully. Do not misuse the service, disrupt it, or infringe other people's rights.
  • You retain responsibility for the content you submit, such as reviews and posts, and must have the right to share it.
  • PopcornBits may restrict accounts that seriously violate these conditions. You may stop using the service and delete your account from Settings.

This is a plain-language summary of the key conditions for using PopcornBits.

PopcornBits uses information to provide and maintain your account and its movie and community features.

  • Signup information includes your name, username, email, country, and date of birth. Your date of birth is used to check the 18+ requirement.
  • Information you add to PopcornBits—such as profile details, reviews, diary entries, watchlists, and posts—is stored to provide the features you use.
  • Account and app data is stored using the service's Supabase database and authentication provider.
  • Audience Insights is optional. If you opt in, you can withdraw consent anytime in Settings.
  • You can edit profile details or request account deletion through Settings. Deleting your account removes account data as handled by the service.

This is a plain-language summary of PopcornBits' privacy practices, not a complete legal policy.
